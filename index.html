How to play

Use the clues to fill the board and reveal the hidden picture.

  1. Read the clues

    Each number gives the length of a same-color run. Read row clues left to right and column clues top to bottom.

  2. Mind the color gaps

    Different colors may touch. Matching colors need at least one empty square between runs.

  3. Fill in the squares

    Pick a palette color, then tap or click a square. Use × Cross to mark an empty square; switch back to ● Fill to color.

  4. Reveal the picture

    Fill the colored squares to finish the puzzle. Undo a move or use one of your three hints if you get stuck.
